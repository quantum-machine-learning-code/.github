## What We Do

This organization hosts **reproducible, verifiable** quantum machine learning experiments at the intersection of quantum computing and AI: embedding parameterized quantum circuits inside classical deep-learning training loops and comparing them against matched-scale classical models under identical configurations.

**One standalone repository per direction** — MIT-licensed, self-contained, and runnable after cloning on its own.

| Direction | Repository | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quantum vision classification | [quantum-mnist](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-mnist) | Variational quantum classifiers (VQC / local Patch VQC / hybrid CNN+VQC / quantum-kernel QSVC) on MNIST handwritten digits, paired against classical MLP / CNN / SVM baselines |
| Quantum + symbolic reasoning / language modeling | [quantum-llm](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm) | Parameterized quantum circuits as **one differentiable layer**: Task 1 is mod-112 addition (117-token vocabulary, zero-overlap compositional generalization test); Task 2 is a character-level autoregressive LM (including $k$-gram entropy floor proofs, multi-observable readouts, and causal context mixing at toy scale) |

## Principles

1. **No claims of quantum advantage.** At this scale, quantum models frequently tie or lag classical baselines; both quantum and classical numbers are reported side by side across both repositories.
2. **State boundaries clearly.** State-vector simulation hits a $2^n$ memory wall, keeping 4–12 qubits in laptop comfort territory; viable quantum LLMs do not exist under current hardware or simulation constraints. `quantum-llm` is a **toy-scale differentiable-layer validation** keeping the direction name—it is not a conversational LLM, and Task 1 mod-112 addition is likewise a toy-scale layer validation rather than an arithmetic solver.
3. **Reproducible, and admit mistakes.** Fixed seeds, offline data, exact reproduction commands, and multi-seed mean ± SD for conclusive claims. Both `quantum-mnist` and `quantum-llm` underwent systematic methodology reviews that uncovered and fixed seed/batch confounds, single-step evaluation noise, and memoryless flattening (`(B, T, Q) -> (B*T, Q)`), documented publicly in each repo's `docs/REVIEW.md`. Round-8 external audits on 2026-11-07 added four tightenings: "quantum kernels equal classical quadratic kernels", "PatchVQC quantum-layer ablation ties", "only 2/20 paired tests remain significant after Bonferroni correction", and "LLM validation set has 90.2% context overlap with training" ([`quantum-mnist/docs/REVIEW.md` Section 15](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-mnist/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md), [`quantum-llm/docs/REVIEW.md` Section 7](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md)). The 2026-11-13 independent corpus rerun cut $k=4$ overlap from 90.2% to 38.7% and saw the quantum training advantage vanish and flip sign on independent sentences (both val CIs cross 0; [`quantum-llm/docs/REVIEW.md` Section 8](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md)). The 2026-11-13 robustness rerun expanded this to 20 seeds × 1200 steps: the generalization-gap difference grew from `+0.1433` to `+0.3263` (Holm-significant), with quantum val loss rising monotonically while train loss kept dropping—ruling out underfitting—though it did not replicate on an extreme-shift synthetic corpus (Section 9).
4. **No quantum hardware required.** All experiments run on classical simulators without IBM Quantum accounts or tokens.

## Tech Stack

**PennyLane** (differentiable quantum circuits) + **PyTorch** (hybrid training), with scikit-learn / SciPy / NumPy / Matplotlib; Python 3.11+; `default.qubit` state-vector simulation with analytic differentiation. Both experiment repositories share core implementations while remaining self-contained so cross-repo dependencies never break reproduction.

## Progress & Roadmap

### 1. `quantum-mnist` (8 Rounds of Empirical Measurement & Attribution)
- **Completed (Rounds 1–7)**: Uncompressed amplitude encoding comparison, input-dimension vs circuit-depth capacity scan, methodology review, 5-seed controlled experiments with paired CIs (`train_multiseed.py`), improved MLP heads and output scaling, 9-group × 5-seed bottleneck decomposition with frozen readout probes (`diagnose_ceiling.py`), 11-group × 5-seed multi-observable / re-uploading / separate LR study (`break_readout.py`), **full two-body correlation readouts (up to 165 dims)**, **information ceiling decomposition** (`probe_information.py`), **9-group × 5-seed widened readout + data scaling** (`push_ceiling.py`), **matched data / near-matched parameter classical baselines** (`classical_budget.py`), **6-round remaining-gap attribution** (`why_low_acc.py`), and **full MNIST (60000/10000) runs** (`train_full_mnist.py`).
- **Breaking the Readout Bottleneck & Full MNIST**: 10-dim `PauliZ` readout caps frozen separability at 0.679 (raw pixels 0.855); widening to 40 dims raises 5-seed accuracy `0.6640 → 0.7720`, and to 165 dims (all ZZ/XX/YY two-body correlations) reaches **`0.8450 ± 0.0100`** (paired `+7.60 ± 2.38 pt`), or **`0.8660 ± 0.0185`** with 1800 real images. On full MNIST (60000 train / 10000 test, 3 epochs × 3 seeds), 165-dim quantum reaches **`0.9421 ± 0.0008`** (40-dim: 0.9014) vs matched-budget classical `PCA-165 + MLP` at **`0.9554 ± 0.0028`** (gap narrowed to **1.33 pt**, paired CI `[+0.45, +2.20]`, classical still leads significantly and runs ~1000× faster). A frozen random unitary 165-dim probe scores `0.9440`, matching end-to-end `0.9421`, indicating the remaining gap stems from global homogeneous quadratic forms lacking local spatial inductive bias (1-px shift perturbation equals 1.20× intra-class dispersion). See [`quantum-mnist/docs/REVIEW.md`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-mnist/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md) Sections 10–13.
- **Round 8 Breakthroughs (`resolve_open_issues.py`, closing open issues & adding baselines)**:
  1. **Resolving Hybrid CNN + VQC Training Collapse**: Identified root cause as post-pooling inter-sample feature std (`~0.003`) being 50× smaller than random linear biases without normalization. Using pure serial `BatchNorm1d + Tanh*(π/2) + near-identity init N(0, 0.1²) + 32-dim readout` (**no residual skip connection across the quantum layer**), `hyb_light_stab` improved from `0.5980 ± 0.1062` to **`0.7650 ± 0.0464`** (paired `+16.70 ± 11.70 pt`, uncorrected CI `[+2.18, +31.22]`, 0/5 seed collapses; not significant after 20-test Bonferroni correction), matching the skip-quantum baseline `nq_light` (`0.7440 ± 0.0651`).
  2. **Pérez-Salinas Trainable Data Re-uploading**: Per-layer trainable input scaling and bias $\text{RY}(\omega_{l,i}\tilde{x}_i + \theta_{l,i})$ recovered Z-only accuracy from naive re-uploading `0.5220 ± 0.0652` to **`0.6580 ± 0.0246`** (`+13.60 ± 7.41 pt`, uncorrected), and with 40-dim readout reached **`0.7470 ± 0.0214`** (`+11.50 ± 4.65 pt` over single upload `a10_z`, uncorrected; neither significant after Bonferroni correction).
  3. **Analytic Kernel (QSVC) Third Baseline**: 5-seed accuracy is **`0.9210 ± 0.0074`** for 10-qubit global amplitude fidelity kernel `qkernel_amp` and **`0.9310 ± 0.0164`** for 8-qubit local 9-patch fidelity kernel `qkernel_patch`. **Audit correction**: Both are classically computable analytic quadratic kernels (`qkernel_amp` matches unscaled classical poly-2 identically across 5/5 seeds), whereas `svm_poly2` `0.8980` used a different preprocessing pipeline (`StandardScaler`) and is not comparable; these must not be framed as quantum kernel superiority or conflated with end-to-end quantum models. See [`docs/REVIEW.md` Section 15.1](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-mnist/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md).
  4. **Local Spatial Inductive Bias (`PatchVQCClassifier`)**: Splitting $28\times 28$ images into $3\times 3$ overlapping $14\times 14$ patches (stride 7) fed through a single weight-shared 8-qubit amplitude circuit (only 48 quantum params) with relative patch-norm preservation reached **`0.8800 ± 0.0112`** (`+3.20 ± 2.02 pt` over `amp_b165`, `+3.90 ± 1.71 pt` over `ref_pca165`; uncorrected CIs exclude 0, not significant after Bonferroni). **Quantum-layer ablation**: Replacing the 8-qubit circuit with classical `Linear(196,32)+Tanh` with everything else identical scored `0.8920 ± 0.0067` (paired `+1.20 pt`, CI crosses 0) and ran ~50× faster—the gain came from classical 2D local-window inductive bias, with **no verifiable positive quantum-layer contribution**. Its 5.7× speedup over `amp_b165` simply reflects 8-qubit vs 10-qubit state-vector simulation cost. See [`quantum-mnist/docs/REVIEW.md`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-mnist/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md) Sections 14–15.

### 2. `quantum-llm` (Task 1: Mod-112 Addition; Task 2: Character LM Methodology Review)
- **Pinpointing & Proving the Memoryless Bigram Wall**: Initial `TinyLM` flattened `(B, T, Q)` to `(B*T, Q)` position-wise, giving zero cross-position interaction despite `--seq-len 16` (effective context $k=1$). On the built-in 1668-char corpus ($V=31$), the exact $k=1$ conditional entropy floor is **`1.9701` nats (perplexity `7.17`)**, and an exact bigram oracle deterministically enters the `' ' -> 't' -> 'h' -> 'e' -> ' '` loop (`"A qubit can hold the the the..."`). With $k=4$ causal context, the theoretical entropy floor drops to **`0.1000` nats (perplexity `1.11`)**.
- **Fixing Confounds & 5-Seed Controlled Comparison (`diagnose_llm.py`)**: Fixed mismatched batch seeds (`seed` vs `seed+1`), unaligned initial weights, the `iter_batches` off-by-one last-character omission bug, and single-step mini-batch noise; introduced deterministic full-corpus `evaluate_lm` and a held-out sentence set (`_HELDOUT_VAL_CORPUS`, 382 chars, which shares 90.2% $k=4$ context overlap with the training corpus and is not an independent OOD split):
  - **Token mode ($k=1$)**: Fixing logit contraction (`scale=5.0` + `tanh*π/2`) and expanding 4-qubit readout from 4-dim `PauliZ` to 48-dim full two-body Pauli observables (`z+x+y+zzall+xxall+yyall`) lowered `tok_q_rich48` train loss from `2.3864 ± 0.0553` (ppl `10.89`) to **`2.0631 ± 0.0148` (ppl `7.87`)**, approaching the $k=1$ floor `1.9701` nats.
  - **Causal context mode ($k=4$, left-padded `CausalContextEncoder`)**: `caus_q_rich48` (4639 params) reached train loss **`0.8271 ± 0.0717` nats (ppl `2.29 ± 0.16`)** and held-out val loss **`1.4494 ± 0.1290` nats (ppl `4.29 ± 0.57`)**, outperforming `caus_identity` (train `1.6549` / val `1.9394`) and matched-budget classical `caus_classical` (4867 params, train `1.1483 ± 0.0617` / val `1.6266 ± 0.1166`; paired val diff `-0.1772` nats, 95% CI `[-0.4019, +0.0475]` crosses 0; and 90.2% $k=4$ overlap means `val PPL 4.29` is not independent generalization evidence), while breaking out of the `"the the the"` loop. **Independent corpus rerun (2026-11-13, [`independent_corpus.py`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/experiments/independent_corpus.py))**: Using 44 training sentences / 14 topic-disjoint validation sentences (3171 / 947 chars, $k=4$ overlap **38.7%**, 69.3% novel 4-grams, 0 UNK) with identical model settings and 5 seeds: `caus_q_rich48` train **`2.1045 ± 0.0200`** vs `caus_classical` `2.2115 ± 0.0169` (paired `−0.1069`, Bonferroni-significant over 4 tests), while independent val was `2.5756 ± 0.0692` vs **`2.4782 ± 0.0385`** (paired `+0.0974`, CI crosses 0), with generalization gap `0.4711` vs `0.2667` (diff **`+0.2044`**, Bonferroni-significant). **The quantum layer's reproducible advantage is in training fit only, not generalization**; 90.2% overlap had disguised the old val set as generalization evidence. Archive: [`docs/independent_corpus_metrics.json`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/independent_corpus_metrics.json); details: [`quantum-llm/docs/REVIEW.md`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md) Sections 7–8. **Robustness rerun (20 seeds × 1200 steps, [archive](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/robustness_metrics.json))**: The generalization-gap difference widened from `+0.1433` (300 steps) to `+0.3263` (1200 steps, Holm-significant); the 5-seed `+0.2044` was a small-sample overestimate. Quantum val loss rose monotonically from `2.5305` to `2.7431` while train loss dropped from `2.2982` to `1.8922`, **ruling out 300-step underfitting and confirming worsening overfitting**. Under 20 seeds, the val difference also became significant (`+0.0599`, Holm `p=0.0022`). See Section 9.

### 3. Honest Boundaries & Next Steps
- **Still no claims of quantum advantage**: On full MNIST, classical still leads by 1.33 pt and is ~1000× faster, and `PatchVQC` gains stem from classical 2D sliding-window locality and quadratic fidelity features. In `quantum-llm`, the 48-dim two-body Pauli readout equals a fixed degree-2 polynomial kernel expansion over 4-qubit causal features and state-vector simulation is ~40× slower than the classical layer. In Task 1, 0/3 primary tests are Holm-significant (quantum val accuracy is `+4.51 pt` higher, but paired CI `[-0.0003, +0.0905]` crosses 0). All results use analytic `default.qubit` simulation without hardware state-prep or finite-shot costs.
- **Round-8 external audits tightened four conclusions**: (1) `qkernel_amp` / `qkernel_patch` are classically computable analytic quadratic kernels, not quantum advantage; (2) replacing `PatchVQC`'s quantum circuit with a classical linear projection yields `0.8920 ± 0.0067` vs quantum `0.8800 ± 0.0112` (CI crosses 0, ~50× faster), showing no verifiable quantum contribution; (3) Bonferroni correction reduced 13/20 significant paired tests to 2/20; (4) the original `quantum-llm` corpus repeated 417 chars 4× and shared 90.2% $k=4$ validation overlap, so `val PPL 4.29` reflects in-distribution recombination only. See each repo's `docs/REVIEW.md`.
- **Independent corpus evidence chain completed (2026-11-13, formerly Priority 1)**: Sentence-disjoint, topic-separated rerun cut $k=4$ overlap from 90.2% to **38.7%**. Result: quantum fits training data significantly better (`−0.1069` nats), classical scores better numerically on independent val (`+0.0974` nats, CI crosses 0), and quantum exhibits a significantly larger generalization gap (`+0.2044` nats, Bonferroni-significant). **Neither quantum advantage nor universal quantum disadvantage is claimed**, and character-level English still shares 38.7% functional 4-grams rather than being strict OOD. Also fixed `audit_corpus.py`'s `max_context` parameter bug and re-archived `corpus_audit.json`.
- **Robustness rerun partially completed (2026-11-13, formerly Priority 3)**: Expanded independent corpus to **20 seeds × 1200 steps** and added a controlled `synthetic` corpus (`k=4` coverage **0.24%**, char unigram JS **0.2538**, ~27× that of `independent`). Results: (1) 5-seed gap `+0.2044` refined to 20-seed `+0.1433` (the seed 0–4 subset reproduces the exact original value, confirming pure sampling variance); (2) longer training widens the gap diff to `+0.3263` (Holm-significant), **not an underfitting artifact**; (3) at 20 seeds, val diff on `independent` is significant (`+0.0599`, Holm `p=0.0022`). However, on `synthetic` both arms collapse above 5 nats val loss and the gap diff `+0.5602` (CI `[-0.1406, +1.2609]`) crosses 0—**conclusions are corpus-bounded and not generalized to "quantum always generalizes worse"**. 35/51 tests survive Bonferroni and 4/6 primary tests survive Holm; both arms used identical un-tuned hyperparameters (`lr=0.015`). See [`docs/REVIEW.md` Section 9](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md) and [`docs/robustness_metrics.json`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/robustness_metrics.json).
- **Task 1: Mod-112 Addition (2026-11-13)**: Added an **exhaustive, zero-overlap** symbolic task as Task 1 of `quantum-llm` to eliminate small-corpus context overlap. Vocabulary **117 = 112 digit tokens + 4 operators (`+ - * /`) + `=`**, encoding `111+1=0` (wrap-around) and `0+0=0` (identity) as 5 tokens with 112-way answer classification (chance `1/112`). All 12544 ordered `(a,b)` pairs are strictly pair-disjoint: 10035 train / 2509 val, with **0.0% $k=4$ validation context coverage** (while $k=1$/$k=2$ coverage is 100%: every token seen, zero pairs seen). At 5 seeds × 2000 steps: quantum `add_q_rich48` unseen-pair val **`0.1817 ± 0.0423`** vs classical ablation `add_classical` **`0.1365 ± 0.0410`**; both exceed chance ($p\approx 0$), yet accuracy remains 18%/14% with rising curves (**task far from solved**). Val accuracy diff `+0.0451` (95% CI `[-0.0003, +0.0905]`) crosses 0, **0/3 primary tests Holm-significant**; `val_loss` is Bonferroni-significant but secondary—**no verifiable positive quantum contribution**. Two essential caveats: under random embeddings across 3 learning rates and 25.5 epochs both arms stalled at chance, so a **shared classical circular angle prior** ($\sin/\cos(2\pi n/112)$) was added (not a quantum advantage), and neither arm was individually tuned. Task 1's generalization-gap diff `−0.0023` (CI crosses 0) contrasts with Task 2 Section 9's `+0.3263`, reinforcing that worse quantum generalization is not universal across tasks. See [`docs/REVIEW.md` Section 10](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md) and [`docs/addition_metrics.json`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/addition_metrics.json).
- **Task 1 3-Mode Embedding Ablation (2026-11-13)**: Clarified that circular angle encoding is an **initialization**, not frozen—under default `trainable_circular`, embeddings already drifted (RMS `0.2816` / `0.3674` over 2000 steps). Under unified 5-seed × 2000-step runs across `frozen_circular` / `trainable_circular` / `trainable_random` (the first two sharing bitwise-identical initial weights differing only in `requires_grad`): frozen scores quantum val `0.2418 ± 0.0488` / classical `0.1839 ± 0.0427`, above default `0.1835 ± 0.0463` / `0.1462 ± 0.0447`; unfreezing diffs `−0.0583` CI `[-0.1318, +0.0153]` and `−0.0377` CI `[-0.1187, +0.0433]` match in sign across both arms but **cross 0 (not significantly harmful)**. Pure random embeddings remain stuck at `loss ≈ ln(112) = 4.735` (`val 0.0062`), proving the circular prior is decisive (`+0.1773` / `+0.1400`, CIs exclude 0). **0/9 primary tests survive Holm**—freezing or unfreezing does not alter "no verifiable positive quantum contribution", and design choice magnitude (−5.8 pt) equals the inter-arm diff (+5.8 pt). Because 2000 steps is only ~6.4 epochs with rising curves, "frozen is better" has an explicit training-budget boundary. See [`docs/REVIEW.md` Section 11](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md) and [`docs/embedding_ablation_metrics.json`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/embedding_ablation_metrics.json).
- **Task 1 Grokking Long-Training Scan (2026-11-13)**: Pair-disjoint `(a,b)` holdout with trainable random embeddings matches the original **grokking** setup of Power et al. (2022, [arXiv:2201.02177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02177)), while Section 10's circular encoding injects the exact $\sin/\cos$ Fourier representation learned at convergence in Nanda et al. (2023, [arXiv:2301.05217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05217)). Ran a 1-seed pilot (19 conditions × 20000 steps) → 5-seed confirmation (40000 steps) → limited-budget quantum illustration, alongside a Liu et al. (2022, [arXiv:2205.10343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10343)) **quadratic-MLP positive control** and Fourier progress measure. **Grokking reproduced, but not in this repo's model**: the positive control at 20% train data + AdamW `wd=1.0` grokked on 5/5 seeds (memorized by step 1000 → abrupt generalization at step 1500 with single-interval jump `+0.667`, val **`0.9944 ± 0.0006`**), whereas on the exact same task, random embeddings, and split, extending `RichQuantumLM` 20× to **40000 steps** across 6 weight-decay levels produced **0/5 grokking seeds** (`val 0.0255 ± 0.0411`). Same-split architecture gap is `+0.9910` CI `[+0.9877, +0.9943]`, the **only Holm-significant primary endpoint** (1/3). Thus "train longer and it will grok" is false: the bottleneck is **missing multiplicative interaction / Fourier representational capacity**, not training budget or gradient dilution. Additionally, frozen vs trainable did not cross over by step 40000 (`0.4207` vs `0.2736`, `p=0.041`, Holm `p=0.082` NS), and first-4-dim phase preservation stayed `1.000` vs decaying to `0.614` (exploratory correlation `r=0.69` with accuracy), confirming Section 11's mechanism. Quantum step time measured `0.1264 s/step` vs classical `0.0038 s/step` (**33.6×**; `3.5 h` vs `0.1 h` per $10^5$ steps; ran 2500 steps = 2.5% as cost illustration only). Caveats: 1-seed pilot has selection bias; phase transition occurred under 80%-holdout split while clean architecture comparison used the 0.2 split; transition here occurs at 500–1500 steps (orders of magnitude faster than transformers, analogous in phenomenon). See [`docs/REVIEW.md` Section 12](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/REVIEW.md) and [`docs/grokking_metrics.json`](https://github.com/quantum-machine-learning-code/quantum-llm/blob/main/docs/grokking_metrics.json).
- **Next Steps (Priority Order)**: (1) **Give `RichQuantumLM` a multiplicative-interaction frontend** (quadratic features / attention) and rerun the random-embedding grokking test—Section 12 pinpointed capacity as the bottleneck; (2) run **per-arm tuned hyperparameter comparisons** for Task 1 and Task 2 independent/1200-step scans (shared `lr=0.015` remains an open confound); (3) perform quantum-layer ablation inside an architecture capable of grokking before assessing quantum impact on phase transitions; (4) Task 1 `holdout_operand` numerical extrapolation and subtraction/multiplication (`/` must handle $\mathbb{Z}_{112}$ zero divisors), plus real external **natural** language corpora for Task 2 with pre-registered primary endpoints; (5) expand full-MNIST comparisons to $\ge 20$ seeds with multiple-comparison correction, and benchmark `PatchVQCClassifier` and Nyström `qkernel_patch` on full MNIST; (6) explore Quantum Attention scoring, finite-shot (3 QWC measurement bases) / depolarizing noise benchmarks, and push quantum runs past $10^4$ steps.

## Contributing

Issues and PRs are welcome. New experiments must retain classical baselines and quantum-free (`Identity`) ablations, fix random seeds **before model construction**, and document full setups, boundaries, and complete outcomes in README and `docs/REVIEW.md`—never cherry-pick favorable runs. Conclusive claims require multi-seed means and confidence intervals (note small-sample effect-size inflation and re-report after expanding seeds); report Bonferroni/Holm-corrected counts when testing $\ge 10$ pairs; report n-gram context overlap with training sets when claiming validation generalization; back "quantum kernel" or "quantum layer helps" claims with identically preprocessed classical kernels and quantum-layer ablations; report independent validation loss and generalization gaps alongside training improvements; and extend training to rule out underfitting before claiming worse quantum generalization, stating corpus boundaries explicitly. Both experiment repositories are MIT-licensed.
